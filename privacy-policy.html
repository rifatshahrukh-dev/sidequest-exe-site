Privacy Policy for SideQuest.EXE

Last updated 6 November 2026

Who we are. SideQuest.EXE is a private tool operated by one individual creator (contact: rifatshahrukhsq@gmail.com) to manage the creator's own channels and accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram.

What it accesses. YouTube Data API v3: only the creator's own two channels; it uploads the creator's videos and reads comments and public statistics on those videos. TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API): only the creator's own two accounts; it confirms which account is connected and uploads the creator's own videos to the creator's inbox as drafts. It does not access any other user's account and is not available to the public.

How viewer comments are used. Comments are read only to count A/B votes. Comment text is processed in memory and is not stored. Only aggregate totals (for example "62% chose A") are kept. We do not collect names, email addresses or other personal information from viewers.

Sharing. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Security. Access credentials are kept in a private repository and are never published.

YouTube and Google. Use of YouTube API Services: the tool uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Access can be revoked at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

TikTok. The tool uses the TikTok API under the TikTok Terms of Service. Access can be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings, Security and permissions, Apps and services.

Data deletion and questions. Because we do not store viewer data, there is nothing to delete. Questions: rifatshahrukhsq@gmail.com

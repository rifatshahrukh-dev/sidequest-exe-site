SideQuest.EXE

SideQuest.EXE is an interactive animated-story series. Each episode ends with a choice, A or B, and viewers vote in the comments. The next episode follows the winning choice.

This page describes a small private tool used only by the creator to manage their own accounts: YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram. It uploads the creator's own videos and counts A/B votes from comments on those videos. It is not offered to other users.

YouTube: English · Bangla
TikTok: English · Bangla

Use of YouTube API Services: the tool uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The tool also uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API, under the TikTok Terms of Service.
